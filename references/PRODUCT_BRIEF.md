# Product brief and page plan

## Confirmed product facts

| Item | Format | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Practical AI for Nigerian Small Businesses | 44-page illustrated PDF | Main paid product |
| Business & Content Planner | 8-page fillable and printable PDF; 67 fields and checkboxes | Free bonus with the main guide |
| The 30-Day Content Campaign | 21-page PDF | Optional separately priced upsell |

The source bundle was prepared on 22 October 2026. Its examples cover several kinds of Nigerian small businesses. The product is a practical reading-and-doing guide, not a hosted video course.

The main guide covers business briefs, reusable prompts, captions, descriptions, customer replies, content planning, graphics, image generation, Canva layout, carousels, Google Flow video guidance, storyboards, editing, administration, proposals and a seven-day practice plan. It has a clickable contents page and official source links.

The companion develops a month of planning, asset production, customer handover and review. The bonus can be filled in a compatible PDF reader or printed. A buyer should save and reopen a test entry before relying on form support in their chosen reader.

## Buyer and goal

Primary visitor: a Nigerian small-business owner who needs clearer, more consistent content and wants practical help using AI without learning technical jargon.

Examples: food vendor, beauty professional, clothing seller, local retailer, freelance service provider and creative business owner. Include a mix; do not imply one industry is the only intended audience.

Primary conversion: continue to the confirmed Selar checkout for the main PDF and its included planner. Secondary conversion: optionally buy the companion through its separate confirmed checkout. Do not require the companion for the main purchase.

## Creative direction

An independent business publisher: confident, useful, calm and specific. Use the coordinated green/cream/gold identity of the PDFs. Let real covers and sample pages establish quality. Avoid neon robots, speculative AI dashboards, decorative counters and generic SaaS pricing tables.

Use “Practical AI” as a short product masthead and make the complete product title unmistakable in the hero or offer. Do not invent a publisher brand or author biography. The lifestyle image depicts an illustrative fictional business owner; it is not proof of a customer, author, endorsement or documented outcome.

## Source-grounded claims

Safe: “44-page illustrated PDF”; “writing, graphics and video guidance”; “worked examples and reusable prompts”; “includes a fillable planner”; “Google Flow guidance”; “examples for Nigerian small businesses”.

Do not use: “unlimited free AI videos”; “master AI in one day”; “guaranteed sales”; “tested on thousands of businesses”; “official Google training”; “Meta-approved”; “live screenshots throughout”; “earn ₦X”; “all three books included” under the default offer.

The Flow chapter discusses limited free access and changing allowances. Avoid quoting a current daily credit number unless it is independently checked and dated before publication.

The guide's screenshot of Flow's public free-credit panel is real. Other interface pictures are simplified teaching diagrams. Storyboard stills are planning illustrations, not actual generated Flow videos.
