## FAQs

**Is this suitable for beginners?**
The guide explains practical tasks through prompts, worked examples and visual guidance. You can start with one task and build from there.

**Is it only for fashion businesses?**
No. It covers tasks that apply across food, retail, beauty, services, freelance work and other small businesses.

**Can I read it on my phone?**
You can read the PDF in a suitable reader on your phone. A larger screen may be more comfortable for some design and video tasks. Fillable-planner support depends on the PDF reader you use.

**Do I need to pay for AI tools?**
The guide includes ways to start with free access where available. Features, credits and limits vary by tool and can change. Buying the PDF does not include a paid software subscription.

**Does it cover graphics and video?**
Yes. It includes image prompting, graphic layout and Canva guidance, plus storyboarding and Google Flow video guidance. It is a PDF guide, not a collection of video lessons.

**Is the 30-Day Content Campaign included?**
The main purchase includes the 44-page guide and the free 8-page planner. The 21-page campaign companion is an optional separate purchase.
